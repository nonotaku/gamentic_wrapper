## Verdict
**8.5/10 as a game — but likely un-featurable as-is.** Buckshot Roulette is a genuinely excellent piece of design: a tense, atmospheric, mechanically tight horror duel that earned its 4.8★ honestly. However, the itch release is a **$2.99 paid download for Windows/Linux (Vulkan-required)** — not a browser HTML5 title, and there is no free web build. If your portal serves embeddable HTML5 games, this cannot be dropped in; you'd only be linking out to a paid store page. Feature it as an *external spotlight* if that fits; skip it if the portal is strictly playable-in-browser.

## Gameplay
You sit across a table from a sinister AI "Dealer" and load a shotgun with a known mix of live and blank shells. Each turn you choose: shoot the Dealer, or shoot *yourself* — self-shooting a blank costs no turn and is the core risk/reward hook. The loop is **press-your-luck probability management** layered with consumable items (magnifying glass to inspect a shell, beer to eject one, cigarettes to heal, handcuffs to skip the Dealer's turn, saw to double damage). What makes the genre tick is *transparent odds under pressure*: nothing is hidden or unfair, so every death feels like your miscalculation, and items turn raw luck into a bluffing/optimization puzzle.

## Why it ranks
It nails a single idea with total polish. The diegetic, near-UI-less presentation and grimy underground-club aesthetic build dread cheaply and effectively. Sessions are 15–20 minutes — perfect for streaming and clips, which drove huge viral reach. The "fair AI that only reads the table" design earns player trust, and the low price plus high craft makes 4.8★ from ~2,900 ratings unsurprising.

## Design notes
Expect minimal HUD, strong audio-driven tension, and elegant onboarding through play rather than tutorials. **No ads, no microtransactions, no dark patterns** — it's a clean paid product. Watch-outs for *your* context: (1) it's a **paid download**, not free/browser; (2) **platform gating** — Windows/Linux + Vulkan, no macOS, no mobile; (3) horror/gun-suicide framing warrants a mature content tag; (4) the itch build lacks the Steam version's multiplayer/leaderboards, and comments show friction over "rebuying" on Steam.
