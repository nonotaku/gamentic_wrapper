## Verdict
**8/10 — worth featuring, with one caveat.** *That's Not My Neighbor* is a genuinely excellent, well-loved spot-the-difference horror sim: tight loop, strong atmosphere, and streamer-proven virality behind its 4.7★. The caveat is a fit problem, not a quality one — the free itch build is a **downloadable Godot PC game (Windows/Mac/Linux), not a browser HTML5 title**. Your portal and its current library are all lightweight in-browser games, so unless a web build exists, this is a "link out to itch" feature rather than an embeddable one. Feature it for the recommendation; don't expect it to run inline.

## Gameplay
You're a doorman for the 1955 Doppelganger Detection Department. Visitors arrive at your window; you cross-check their **photo, name, and stated apartment** against a paper file, then buzz them in or reject them. Some are doppelgangers — subtly wrong faces, mismatched names, off details, or outright monstrous tells. Get it wrong and you die. It's the *Papers, Please* bureaucratic-scrutiny loop reskinned as jump-scare horror: the tension comes from second-guessing tiny discrepancies under time pressure while the "people" get progressively less human. Mouse-only, ~30-minute sessions, escalating difficulty as more rules stack.

## Why it ranks
Three things. **Streamability** — reading faces aloud and screaming at a botched call is perfect content; it went Steam/streamer-scale from exactly this. **Instant readability** — the rules are obvious in ten seconds, but mastery isn't, the ideal itch hook. **Novelty + polish** — it fused a respected mechanic (document-checking) with a fresh horror frame and shipped it clean in Godot. 4.7★ from 1,582 ratings reflects a game that overdelivers on a free download.

## Design notes
Expect: single-window UI, drag/click document comparison, a rulebook that grows, and horror escalation via faces and audio stings. It's **color-blind friendly with subtitles** — good accessibility to advertise. No ads or dark patterns; monetization is honest (free legacy v1.x on itch, optional name-your-price, a $2.99 v2.0 remake on Steam). Watch-outs: **horror/jump-scare content warning** for your portal; and confirm the build format before listing — the paid v2.0 reworks mechanics, so review whichever version you actually feature.
