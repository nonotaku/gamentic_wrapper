## Gameplay
You wake in your bedroom to a tall, multi-eyed entity and must "date" her to survive. The core loop is pure branching dialogue: read a beat, pick a response, and an **Interest Meter** silently tallies your choices toward one of **10 endings** (romance, doom, and shades between). A single run is ~15–20 minutes; the hook is replaying to unlock all endings. What makes this genre tick is exactly that tension between curiosity and consequence — the player treats dialogue as a puzzle with a hidden scoring rubric, and the horror framing makes every "wrong" flirt feel risky.
